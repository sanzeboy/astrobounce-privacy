# Astro Bounce – Privacy Policy

*Effective 23 November 2026*

Astro Bounce is a single-player space brick-breaker game for Android made by Sanjeeb Boyaju ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to **sanjeebboyaju@gmail.com**.

## What the game stores about you

**Your game progress.** Your best wave, your ore, the skins you own, your missions, your daily streak, your saved run and your settings (sound, music, vibration and aiming style). This is saved in a file on your device only. It is not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see it.

**No account with us.** Astro Bounce has no account of its own. We do not ask for your name, your email address or any other contact details.

We do not collect your contacts, your photos, your files, your microphone or camera, or your precise location. The game has no chat, no online multiplayer and no in-app purchases.

## Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), your scores and your achievements are processed by Google to show them to you and on leaderboards. This is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive your Google account details.

## Advertising

Astro Bounce is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward (continuing a run or doubling your ore). There are no banner ads.

To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), information about your device, and information about how you interact with the ads, as well as crash and performance information about the ads SDK. Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. This information goes to Google, not to us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before AdMob uses your information for personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in **Settings › Privacy options**. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Service providers

- **Google:** AdMob advertising, Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements, and Google Play for distributing the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Unity Technologies:** the game is built with the Unity engine. The engine itself may collect basic, non-identifying crash and device information. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

We do not sell your information. We do not receive or store any personal information ourselves.

## How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Both of these delete it for good. Advertising data and Play Games data are kept by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above, and manage your Google data at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

## Children

Astro Bounce is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want related advertising data removed, contact us and we will help.

## Security

Information sent between the game and Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
